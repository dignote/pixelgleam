# Artwork Normalization / Edit Prompt

Refine the supplied PixelGleam master artwork for production topology while preserving its identity, pose and composition.

Do NOT redesign the subject.

Normalize only:
- reduce tiny decorative fragments
- simplify uncontrolled gradients into intentional highlight/base/shadow groups
- strengthen ambiguous boundaries between major parts
- keep meaningful negative spaces open
- remove hairline gaps that are accidental, but preserve visually intentional gaps
- remove noisy texture that would create micro semantic islands
- keep the artwork attractive and polished

Do not add a grid, numbers, visible polygons or UI.
