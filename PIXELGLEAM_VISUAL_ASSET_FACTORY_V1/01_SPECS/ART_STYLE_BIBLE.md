# PixelGleam Art Style Bible V1

## Product visual identity
Gem Mosaic Atelier / Soft Faceted Resin Gem.

Personality: relaxing, tactile, crafted, cheerful, light, polished.

## Master illustration rules
- LIGHT background / neutral studio surface.
- Strong readable silhouette.
- Clear part separation.
- Large meaningful color masses.
- Controlled shading rather than continuous noisy gradients.
- Avoid hairline details, dust/noise textures, excessive sparkles and tiny isolated decorations.
- Important holes and spaces between parts must be visually intentional.

## Semantic color budgets
Recommended:
- Simple: 8–12 gameplay semantic colors.
- Standard: 12–20.
- Detailed: 20–28.
- Hard ceiling for routine production: about 32 unless explicitly approved.

Visual RGB shades may exceed the semantic color count. Gameplay semantic colors group related highlights/shadows when appropriate.

## Composition
- Prefer one dominant subject or one coherent scene.
- Keep subject comfortably inside safe margins.
- Avoid many thin crossing elements.
- Avoid dense micro-detail concentrated in one corner.
- Thin elements must be thick enough to support stable topology.

## Final settled gem appearance
The final must sit between two bad extremes:
1. too square / pixel-grid,
2. too smooth / ordinary illustration.

Target:
- organic cell rhythm,
- subtle micro-seams,
- small facet/specular language,
- soft contour preservation,
- consistent material response,
- no casino-like excessive sparkle or chrome.
