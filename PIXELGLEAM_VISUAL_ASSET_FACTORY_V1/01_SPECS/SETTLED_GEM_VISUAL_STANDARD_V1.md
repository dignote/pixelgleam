# Settled Gem Visual Standard V1

## Role
`settled_texture.png` is the final completed visual truth for an artwork.

## Material direction
Soft Faceted Resin Gem:
- polished but not metallic,
- restrained specular highlight,
- shallow tactile depth,
- small local value variation,
- subtle seams/cell rhythm,
- soft, friendly casual-game material.

## Must preserve
- approved master silhouette,
- semantic color intent,
- facial readability on characters,
- meaningful negative spaces,
- recognizable category/subject at thumbnail size.

## Must avoid
- uniform hex/pixel texture stamped across everything,
- hard square lattice,
- excessive black grout,
- casino jewel shine,
- noisy sparkle on every cell,
- ordinary smooth illustration with no mosaic readability.

## Runtime relationship
G02 is transient flight/landing feedback. Do not use repeated G02 sprites as authoritative final artwork. The settled texture owns the final appearance.
